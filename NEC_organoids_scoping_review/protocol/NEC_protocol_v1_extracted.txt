Structured abstract
Background. Experimental neonatal necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) has been studied using intestinal
organoids derived from diverse biological sources. Source history (including clinical NEC diagnosis),
intestinal sampling site and experimentally induced injury may affect interpretation of organoid
models, while assays and validation approaches are heterogeneous.

Objective. To map biological origin, NEC-model construction, organoid-specific molecular mechanisms
and reported validation in primary studies of intestinal organoid models relevant to neonatal NEC.

Methods. This scoping review will use a Population–Concept–Context framework, source-stratified
eligibility and a nested study–source–experiment–readout extraction structure. The review has already

1. Scientific rationale and interpretive scope
Intestinal organoids, enteroids, enterospheres, organoid-derived monolayers and organoid-based
microfluidic systems are used to study NEC-related epithelial injury, barrier disruption, innate immune
signaling, cell death and regeneration. They vary by donor species, developmental age, anatomical
segment, NEC diagnosis, source lesion status, genetics, culture architecture and experimental insult.
The central question is not whether a particular therapy is effective, but how these source
characteristics relate to what has been modelled, measured and validated.

The expression “relate to” is intentionally noncausal. Source effects will only be discussed as directly
comparative findings where studies contain appropriate within-study contrasts; cross-study differences
will be treated as descriptive associations potentially confounded by exposure, age, tissue site and
platform.

2. Review question, PCC and objectives
Primary question: How does the biological origin of intestinal organoids relate to the experimental
modeling of neonatal necrotizing enterocolitis, the molecular mechanisms investigated, and the extent
of reported model validation?

4. Eligibility criteria and evidence strata
4.1 Mandatory platform criterion
Original measurements must involve intestinal epithelial organoids, enteroids or enterospheres, a
monolayer derived from such a culture, or an organ-on-chip system with an explicitly organoid-
derived intestinal epithelial component. Conventional immortalized cell lines without an organoid
arm, animal NEC experiments without an organoid arm, reviews, purely computational reanalyses and
nonintestinal organoids alone are excluded.

The origin of parallel rat/mouse experiments must not be assigned to human organoids. A genetic
knockout is a property of the source model, not a mutually exclusive additional species category.

4.4 Evidence type and restrictions
No language, publication date, species, molecular target or treatment filters will be used for eligibility.
Full peer-reviewed primary reports are the principal synthesis. Preprints and congress abstracts are
identified in auxiliary strata; final publications and overlapping reports are linked by study family.
Non-English texts will be assessed with translation when feasible. Full-text retrieval failures will be
documented without converting abstract-only findings into verified organoid measurements.

5. Information sources and search chronology
Sources comprise MEDLINE via PubMed, Embase via Embase.com, Scopus and Web of Science Core
Collection. Main searches combine NEC and organoid/enteroid/chip terminology. Complementary
searches combine NEC and intestinal epithelium/stem-cell/model terms to identify articles that do not
name organoids in indexed fields. Backward and forward citation searching is planned. All source
exports, dates, interface details and exact strings as actually run will be preserved; revised harmonized
strings in Appendix A are not retrospectively represented as the strings used for prior exports.

Review chronology: prior to this version, bibliographic searches and preliminary deduplication were
performed and an assistant-assisted title/abstract classification produced a working register of 104
candidate records, including 55 provisional CORE records. Several publications were examined
selectively at full text. This stage is exploratory and does not constitute completed dual-reviewer full-
text screening or definitive data extraction. The exact search dates and logs will be reconciled with the
original exports; where unavailable, this limitation will be reported. The protocol deposit will therefore
be explicitly identified as occurring AFTER initial search and screening activity.

Further searches or updates will follow the frozen strategies with deviations and execution dates
logged. The broad complementary stream may have lower precision; records from both streams will be
deduplicated. Citation chasing will be documented with seed studies, platforms and dates. Google
Scholar, if used, will be a bounded supplementary source (first 100 relevance-ranked results per
prespecified query) with ranking limitations disclosed.

6. Screening and adjudication
Two independent human reviewers will reassess candidate records and subsequently retrieved reports
against the frozen criteria. A pilot of at least 25 unique records will be independently screened;
discordances will be discussed, and the screening manual clarified until a prespecified agreement
threshold of at least 75% is reached before continuing. An independent third reviewer will resolve
unresolved disputes. Previous assistant screening will not be represented as a substitute for this
process.

Full-text exclusions will be assigned a primary reason: no intestinal organoid-derived epithelial
experiment; no NEC-directed question; no original biological data; duplicate/overlapping report
without independent experiments; insufficient full-text evidence; other specified reason. Publication,
report, study family, donor line, source arm and experiment will be separately tracked. Final PRISMA-
ScR counts will not be derived from the provisional 55 CORE candidate total.

7. Data charting and quality controls
The extraction hierarchy will be: bibliographic record → study family → biological source/line →
experiment/contrast → analyte/phenotype. Key source and molecular variables will be independently
extracted by two human reviewers or verified by a second reviewer against the exact full-text figure,
table and supplement. Unknown or unreported variables will be coded PENDING, NR, NA or
UNRESOLVED, never silently imputed.

8. Planned synthesis and reporting
The CORE synthesis will be stratified by human NEC donor-derived, non-NEC human donor with
induced injury, fetal/other human models, murine healthy or post-NEC donors, and other species. Co-
primary outcomes P1–P3 will be tabulated in separate but linked matrices. A study with multiple
biological source arms will count once in overall study totals but may appear in multiple source-
specific tables, with overlap disclosed.

Planned displays: PRISMA-ScR flow; source × NEC construct × platform matrix; organoid-specific
target/pathway × assay × experimental perturbation matrix; descriptive validation/replication matrix;
and an evidence-gap map. No pooled efficacy meta-analysis, GRADE treatment certainty, ranking of
“best” organoid source or composite quality score is planned. Where direct head-to-head comparisons
exist, they will be described within the study; cross-study comparisons will remain noncausal.

9. Transparency, ethics, amendments and deposit
This review synthesizes published or lawfully accessed reports and involves no new human or animal
experiment. Publisher-controlled full-text PDFs will not be redistributed. Derived non-infringing data
and search logs may be shared subject to applicable licenses. AI assistance in preliminary bibliographic
triage and drafting will be disclosed and will not replace independent human eligibility decisions.

Zenodo will provide a public, versioned archive and DOI, but not peer-review certification. This deposit
will be a time-stamped PROTOCOL FOR AN ONGOING REVIEW, not a prospective registration before
initial searches. Any subsequent substantive methodological changes will be dated and archived as
amendments; the completed review will cite the exact deposited version DOI.

Appendix C. Review stage and relationship to existing candidate register
The internal register contains 104 provisionally relevant bibliographic records: 55 CORE candidates, 10
conference abstracts, 30 CONTEXTUAL, 4 METHODS and 5 auxiliary records. The 55 CORE include 47
records from initial screening and 8 recovered from reconsideration of 32 uncertain records. These are
not final inclusion counts, independent studies or PRISMA-ScR flow totals. A separate internal
alignment audit identifies possible preprint/full-article overlap and records with uncertain direct NEC
modelling. These results were used to assess feasibility and procedural consistency; they do not alter
eligibility criteria on the basis of observed findings.

A small set of prior records has already been inspected at full-text level for methodological
development. Definitive inclusion/exclusion decisions and extraction remain subject to the planned
independent reviewer process. The date of this protocol must not be represented as preceding the prior
bibliographic searches.
